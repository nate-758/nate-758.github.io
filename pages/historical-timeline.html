Put the events in order

Arrange the events from earliest to latest. Drag cards or use the arrow buttons, then check your order. Each incorrect check costs 10 points; a correct order earns the points you have left, up to 100.

    How to play

    1. Put the events in chronological order.
    2. Drag a card or use its up and down buttons.
    3. Check how many events are in the right place; finish the order to reveal dates and sources.

    The maximum score is 100 points. Each incorrect check costs 10 points; correct checks are never penalized.

    Sources are shown with the answers.
